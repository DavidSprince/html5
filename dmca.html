DMCA Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

ReelsHub Vault respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users of the Site to do the same. We do not host any files ourselves — all download buttons link to third-party hosted resources (Google Drive, Mega.nz, Google Docs, Notion, etc.).

1. Reporting Infringement

If you are a copyright owner, or authorised to act on behalf of one, and believe that a link listed on ReelsHub Vault directs to material that infringes your copyright, please send a notice containing the following information to hello@reelshubvault.com:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • The exact URL(s) on ReelsHub Vault where the allegedly infringing link appears.
  • Your contact information (name, email address, and, if available, phone number).
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorised.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on their behalf.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

2. Our Response

Upon receiving a valid notice, we will promptly remove or disable the specific link in question from our Site listings, typically within 3–5 business days.

3. Counter-Notification

If you believe a link was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice with a detailed explanation to the same email address, and we will review the claim in good faith.

4. Note on Third-Party Hosting

Because the underlying files are hosted on third-party services, removing a link from ReelsHub Vault does not remove the file from its original host. Rights holders may also need to file a separate takedown request directly with the hosting provider.
